Run Basics

Weapons auto-target the closest enemy. Your job is movement: keep lanes open, collect XP when safe, and use dash to pass through pressure instead of saving it forever.

  • XP gems level you up and pause the fight for upgrade picks.
  • Every normal enemy kill gives 1 gold for the music shop.
  • Bosses add score, unlock progress, and force you to reposition.

Strategy Notes

Early runCircle wide, collect small XP clusters, and avoid getting pinned against the arena border.
Level upsPick damage and cooldown early, then add mobility or defensive upgrades when enemies begin to crowd.
Boss wavesWatch for charge warnings, keep dash ready, and clear summoned enemies before they trap your escape path.
